# Pocket

**Peaceful Revolution — Dover**

You want your people alive. That is the whole prize.

Death is a repair problem. Repair needs a civilization that still trades, learns, and does not rule by force. That promise is called Good News. It is closed to wreckers.

Nobody asked you. That noticing is not hatred. A vote is not consent. A crowd is not an owner. A category is not a man.

Name the victim. Name the act. Name the man who caused it. If you cannot, you have no case.

The cabinet takes without asking. That is theft with a letterhead.

A line across the port traps people who did not harm you. That is the same method with a mask.

Self-defence stops the crossing that is happening, aimed at the one doing it, and then it ends.

National socialism is socialism with a blood-story. It is a grave, not a shield.

**Carry this:**

1. Do not do to others what they would not want done to them.
2. No victim, no crime.
3. No one stands above the law. Not them. Not you.
4. Faces on. Port open. Words public.
5. If a line in this pamphlet fails the dictionary, strike the line.

Do not become them.
