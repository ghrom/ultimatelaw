**Peaceful Revolution — Dover**

People you love die. Repair needs a civilization that still trades. That promise is Good News. It is closed to wreckers.

Nobody asked you. A vote is not consent. A crowd is not an owner. A category is not a man.

Name the victim. Name the act. Name the man who caused it. If you cannot, there is no case.

The cabinet takes without asking. That is theft with a letterhead.

Saturday the papers cried blockade. Kent Police nicked nobody. A headline is not a judgment.

National socialism is socialism with a blood-story. It loses.

1. Do not do to others what they would not want done to them.
2. No victim, no crime.
3. No one stands above the law. Not them. Not you.
4. The port stays open. Law is public.
5. If a line fails the dictionary, strike the line.
