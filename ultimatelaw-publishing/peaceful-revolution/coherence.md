# Coherence map

Every load-bearing claim in [`manifesto.md`](manifesto.md) and [`pocket.md`](pocket.md) is mapped to a term in [core.json](https://ultimatelaw.org/core.json) / the Coherent Dictionary. If a mapping fails, strike the line in the pamphlet. That is the falsifiability clause.

Terms below use the project's published definitions, not street slang.

## Words used in the street sense, then pinned

The pamphlet talks to working men. A few ordinary words are used, then bound:

- **Revolution** is not in `core.json` as a term. Here it means a change in the pattern of who an agent treats as law, accomplished in peace. It does not mean seizing a monopoly or organised violence. If it is read as war, the read is wrong and the line is to be struck.
- **Nation / national** appear only inside the rejection of national socialism, as the blood-story stuck onto socialism. They are not a rights-holder. A collective has no rights beyond its members.
- **Port / A20 / Dover / Saturday / boats** are circumstances: facts that fix the meaning of the acts. They do not add consent and do not remove it.
- **Lung** is metaphor for the port's function in trade. The load-bearing claim is the trade/harm row above, not the image.

## What the pamphlet refuses, so it cannot drift

These would break `core.json` and are out of the text on purpose:

- Installing nomocracy by force on men who have not crossed anyone
- Collective blame of "migrants," "the English," or "the government" as a hive
- Treating the blockade as self-defence
- Treating a constitution, a party, or a crowd as standing above the law
- Any claim of group property in "the streets" or "the country" apart from individual title
- Deterrence-by-spectacle (punishing without a victim to frighten others)
- Murder, war, or hunting a type

## Overturn

If any charge in this folder misapplies a definition, the line should be struck and the pamphlet updated. Error is not evil; refusing to correct it is.
