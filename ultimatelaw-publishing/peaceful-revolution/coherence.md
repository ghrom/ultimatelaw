# Coherence map

Every load-bearing claim in [`manifesto.md`](manifesto.md) and [`pocket.md`](pocket.md) is mapped to a term in [core.json](https://ultimatelaw.org/core.json). If a mapping fails, strike the line in the pamphlet.

| Pamphlet claim | Term | Why it holds |
|----------------|------|----------------|
| Death of those you love is the hard problem; only a fast civilization addresses it in this life | Happiness; Good News; Civilizational Velocity | Happiness names that problem. Good News is the technical claim. Velocity is why wrecking cooperation kills people who would have lived. |
| Good News is technical, earned by trade, innovation, consent; closed to coercion and violence | Good News | Direct, including the conditional clause. |
| A port is trade; a living port keeps velocity | Trade; Civilizational Velocity | Trade is a voluntary swap. A port is where that swap happens at scale. |
| Nobody asked; a vote is not consent | Consent; Democracy; Government; Obligation | Consent is free agreement. Democracy picks rules; it cannot create consent. Forced duties are coercion. |
| Taxes and official seizures are theft | Theft; Government; Politician | Theft includes taking by taxes or claimed authority. |
| Entering what a man owns without agreement is a crossing | Property; Ownership; Boundary; Violation; Permission | Title is first use or transfer. Permission is specific. Crossing without it is violation. |
| Being born elsewhere is not a crime | Crime; Victim; Collective Punishment; Collective Responsibility; People | Crime needs a named victim and a caused crossing. Group membership is not causation. |
| Punishing a type is injustice | Collective Punishment; Collective Responsibility; Causation; Correlation | Punishment without individual guilt creates innocent victims. |
| People-smuggling by lies is fraud; forced housing/payment is theft and coercion | Fraud; Deception; Theft; Coercion | Direct. |
| Saturday is not a closed case; no arrests; a headline is not a judgment | Evidence; Crime; Victim; Judgment | Evidence raises or lowers probability. Crime needs a named victim and a named actor. Judgment discovers; it does not invent. Kent Police recorded no arrests. |
| Self-defence is narrow; a crowd is not an owner | Self-Defense; Forfeiture; Collective; Property; Ownership; Conduct | Self-defence aims at the agent now crossing, then ends. A collective has no rights beyond its members. Title does not move by chant. |
| National socialism is rejected | Socialism; Collective Punishment; Government; War; Good News; Way of Happiness | Socialism is forced taking. A blood-story adds collective punishment. A street monopoly is still government. It closes Good News. Reciprocity is the Way. |
| Nomocracy is not installed by seizing the state | Government; Nomocracy; Authority; Hierarchy | Law from logic and reciprocity, not rulers. Seizing the monopoly keeps the monopoly. |
| Peaceful revolution is a change of who you treat as law, without war | Peace (gloss: condition without war or violence); War; Voluntaryism; Nomocracy; Social Construct | Peace forbids war as the method. Forced taxes and borders are forced constructs. The pattern of treated-as-law can change without a new fist. |
| A forced benefit is not a benefit | Benefit; Kindness; Consent | Forced help is harm. |
| Prosecute with named victims, published definitions, proportion, and an overturn line | Crime; Evidence; Judgment; Proportion; Justice; Restitution; Punisher; Mandate | Matches the project's method. Punisher acts only inside a victim's mandate. |
| Office does not wash a crime | Politician; Guilt; Law | Guilt follows causation. |
| Error is not evil; refusing to correct it is | Error; Belief | Direct. |
| This pamphlet is speech, not harm | Freedom of Speech; Expression; Harm | Speech is harm only as deception, threat, or fraud. |
| Reciprocity even when strong | Way of Happiness; Reciprocity; Golden Rule (Passive Version); Perimeter | Direct. Perimeter prosecutes ordinary crimes with living victims; it does not need a single fist. |

## Words used in the street sense, then pinned

- **Revolution** is not a `core.json` term. Here it means a change in the pattern of who an agent treats as law, accomplished in peace. It does not mean seizing a monopoly or organised violence.
- **Nation / national** appear only inside the rejection of national socialism. They are not a rights-holder.
- **Port / Dover / Saturday / boats** are circumstances. They fix meaning. They do not add consent and do not remove it.

## Overturn

If any line in this folder misapplies a definition, strike the line. Error is not evil; refusing to correct it is.
