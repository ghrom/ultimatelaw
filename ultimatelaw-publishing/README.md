# ultimatelaw-publishing

Publishable Ultimate Law content, organized for distribution.

This tree is the drop-in for the private publishing repository. The public framework stays at [github.com/ghrom/ultimatelaw](https://github.com/ghrom/ultimatelaw). Definitions are taken from [ultimatelaw.org/core.json](https://ultimatelaw.org/core.json) and the [Coherent Dictionary](https://ultimatelaw.org/coherent-dictionary-of-simple-english/).

## Contents

### Peaceful Revolution (`peaceful-revolution/`)
A manifesto for men in port towns who already know they were not asked. It starts from [Good News](https://ultimatelaw.org/dictionary/good-news) as survival, not a sermon: keep civilization fast enough to outrun death, and do not become the next crew that rules by force. Written to be adopted. Coherent with `core.json`. Not a party. Not a street army.
