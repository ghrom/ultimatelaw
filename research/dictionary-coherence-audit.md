# Coherent Dictionary — Full Coherence Audit
**File audited:** `dictionary/coherent-dictionary-of-simple-english.txt` (190 entries) and `dictionary/coherent-dictionary-extended.txt` (~52,400 lines)
**Date:** 2026-08-03
**Method:** 7 independent finder passes (4 doctrine clusters + closure analysis + framework-gap analysis + extended-file sync), every contradiction claim adversarially verified against the exact text. 46 raw claims → 27 survived verification (quotes checked, refutation attempted); the rest were dissolved by careful reading and are **not** listed here. Duplicates across clusters merged below.

**Headline:** the dictionary is closer to coherent than the finding count suggests — most attacks on the trade, metaphysics, and political clusters were successfully refuted by the text as written (10–11 of 15 per cluster). The failures concentrate in two places: (1) the **justice cluster**, where newer entries (Justice, Judgment, Mandate, Outlaw, Proportion, Self-Defense) define a refined model that the surrounding entries (Coercion, Violence, Enforcement, Restitution, Crime, Victim, Evil, Murder) were never synced with; and (2) **missing machinery** — concepts the system leans on but never defines.

**Standing constraint:** the Law entry is the framework's fixed point — "it cannot be changed, all the rest is commentary." No finding in this audit may be resolved by editing Law. Where commentary entries diverge from Law, the commentary is what moves; every reconciliation flows toward Law, never through it.

## Part 1 — The root cause: forfeiture is stated but never propagated

The framework's actual device for making punishment non-criminal is **forfeiture**, and it exists in exactly three places:

- Rights: "whoever crosses another's boundary forfeits the protection of their own"
- Proportion: "by taking what was not theirs they forfeit the protection of what is"
- Outlaw: "the outlaw forfeited reciprocal protection by rejecting it" — plus the only appearance of the descriptive-vs-normative victim split ("may create a victim in the descriptive sense … but it is not wrong and creates no crime")

But the entries that *define* the core terms — **Victim, Crime, Harm, Evil, Guilt, Violation, Murder, Coercion** — never carry the qualifier. Read literally:

- Retribution mirrors harm onto a non-consenting offender → by Crime ("An action that harms someone who didn't want it, creating a victim") and Harm, **every punishment is a crime**.
- Evil actively blocks the rescue: "Intentions, beliefs, votes, traditions, or claimed necessity do not negate evil once a victim exists" — forfeiture is not on the list of non-negators.
- Guilt "exists objectively from causation" → **every punisher and every outlaw-ender acquires objective guilt**.
- Coercion says punishment "breaks the Golden Rule" (excepted from *wrongness* only), while Punishment says it is "not breaking fairness but enforcing it" — a flat surface contradiction.
- The axiom "no victim = no crime" quantifies over a Victim definition under which every punished offender is a victim.

**Single highest-leverage fix:** add a **Forfeiture** entry (the mechanism by which crossing another's boundary suspends the protection of one's own, to the extent of the crossing), plus the descriptive/normative victim distinction, and cross-reference it from Victim, Crime, Harm, Evil, Guilt, Violation, Murder, and Coercion. This one change dissolves findings 2.2, 2.3, 2.4 below and most of the Outlaw/Punishment tension.

## Part 2 — Confirmed contradictions (all verifier-confirmed against exact quotes)

### 2.1 CRITICAL — Does restitution erase guilt? Law/Punishment/Mandate vs Justice/Guilt/Crime
- Law: "The purpose of punishment is to erase guilt, via retribution **and restitution**."
- Mandate: proxy closes the moral debt "through retribution, **restitution**, or release."
- Punishment: applies "retribution **or restitution** … its purpose is to close a moral debt."
- Restitution: "Restitution erases debt caused by wrongdoing" (unqualified).
- Against: Justice: "Restitution repairs the material damage **independently**; Justice disposes of the moral debt." Crime: "**Only** Justice … erases the moral debt." Guilt: "erased **only** through Justice." Forgiveness confirms the split (guilt can close while restitution remains owed).
- Litmus test the entries answer oppositely: a thief who suffers full mirrored loss but never returns the goods — guilt erased (Justice) or not (Law/Mandate)?
- **Fix:** Law is fixed and is not a candidate for editing; the commentary must conform to it. Law's "via retribution and restitution" names the two components of the debt punishment closes — restitution the material part, collection or release the moral part. Reword Justice, Crime, Guilt, Punishment, Mandate, and Restitution until each states that split explicitly and none contradicts Law's channel list (e.g. Restitution: "erases the material debt; it does not by itself close the moral debt").

### 2.2 CRITICAL — Victim/Crime/Evil literally make every punishment a crime
See Part 1. Fix by propagating Forfeiture.

### 2.3 CRITICAL — Coercion's sole exception condemns self-defense
- Coercion: "wrong **unless** applied as proportionate **punishment for harm already done**."
- Self-Defense: force against an "ongoing or **immediately credible**" crossing, and "Self-defense is **not punishment**."
- Defensive force satisfies Coercion's definiens (overrides the aggressor's intentions); it is explicitly not punishment; in the imminent branch there is no harm already done. The only stated exception can never apply.
- Adjacent gaps: Violence's "only justified to stop or repair harm **already done**" fails the imminent case (rescuable only via the unstated chain Threat→"already a form of violence"); Mandate grounds the defensive mandate only in "the violation in progress," never the imminent one.
- **Fix:** give Coercion a second exception (minimum force to halt an ongoing or immediately credible crossing, see Self-Defense); add "or immediately credible" to Mandate; state the threat-is-violence-in-progress bridge explicitly.

### 2.4 CRITICAL — Murder's definition captures executions, outlaw-ending, and lethal defense
- Murder: "The deliberate killing of an agent who did not consent to die" — **no carve-out at all** — creating "permanent, uncloseable guilt."
- But Proportion authorizes death as a ceiling, Outlaw authorizes ending, Self-Defense can be lethal. Every executioner/defender/outlaw-ender deliberately kills a non-consenting agent → murderer by the definiens. The framework's reconciler is forfeiture, not consent, and Murder never mentions it.
- **Fix:** build the forfeiture element into Murder's definiens (killing an agent *whose reciprocal protection is intact* and who did not consent).

### 2.5 CRITICAL — Invariant Logic vs universal flux ("emerge" used in two incompatible senses)
- Logic: "unchanging … invariant"; Law: "cannot be changed."
- Infinite Change: "**nothing** stays the same, and from this flux, **logic**, natural laws, and all patterns self-emerge."
- Emergence defines what emerges as "persistent patterns"; Pattern: "**none are permanent**." Chain: logic self-emerges → what emerges is a pattern → no pattern is permanent → logic is not permanent. Contradicts "invariant."
- Timeless Infinity blocks the epistemic escape by putting logic inside the emergent ontology: "From this emerges **everything real, including logic** and fair rules."
- Also: Logic is a "way of thinking" (mind-dependent category) yet mind-independent; Reason has evidence-weighing *uncover* logic although weighing evidence presupposes logic.
- **Fix:** state that "nothing stays the same" quantifies over contents/patterns of the flux, not over the invariant constraints *on* transformation; give Logic that distinct status; split ontological emergence from epistemic discovery and reword Infinite Change / Timeless Infinity / Universe / Reason accordingly.

### 2.6 CRITICAL — Good News is a Prediction/Faith by the dictionary's own taxonomy, labeled "logical extrapolation"
- Good News: "not utopian fantasy but **logical extrapolation**" / "technical immortality bounded by physics, **not faith**" / "The **achievable promise**."
- By its own taxonomy it is a Prediction ("a claim about what will happen based on a model"); the inference from incremental healthspan gains to indefinite life is a non-sequitur without an unstated escape-velocity premise; Fallibility permits civilizational failure. Held as a promise ahead of settling evidence, it matches Faith's definition word-for-word while denying being faith.
- Same laundering: Way of Happiness asserts "No agent stays the strongest forever" as fact (only the epistemic version — "none can know" — is supported); Singleton asserts "whatever endures is selected toward Perimeters" as certainty; Happiness/Eschatology inherit Good News as settled.
- No entry anywhere distinguishes logically necessary from empirical/contingent claims — so "logical extrapolation" is undefined and does illegitimate rhetorical work.
- **Fix:** add the logical-vs-empirical claim distinction as an entry; reclassify the forward-looking claims as conditional Predictions with explicit failure conditions.

### 2.7 MAJOR — Retribution is neither "stop" nor "repair," so Violence and Enforcement never license it
- Violence: "only justified to **stop or repair** harm already done." Enforcement: "only justified to **stop or repair** real harm." But the dictionary's own tripartite (Good, Justice): stop violations / **repair** = restitution / **close** = retribution. Retributive force is therefore definitionally unjustified violence, while Punishment calls it "the enforcement arm of Justice."
- And the legitimacy formula is drawn **four different ways**: Enforcement "stop or repair" / Nomocracy "**prevent** or repair" / Regulation & Government "**only repair**" / Violence "stop or repair already done." Nomocracy's "prevent" is the outlier that licenses preventive rules — exactly what Perimeter ("does not preempt"), Self-Defense, and Regulation forbid; a hostile reader drives regulation straight through it.
- Also: Enforcement's genus ("use of force **or threat of force** to make rules obeyed") is definitionally Deterrence, and per Threat the standing threat is itself violence; no entry draws the legitimate-enforcement-threat vs condemned-deterrence line.
- **Fix:** one canonical formula everywhere: "stop harm in progress (including immediately credible), repair damage done (restitution), or close a moral debt within the victim's mandate and proportion (retribution)" — and a sentence in Deterrence distinguishing standing commitment to post-hoc justice from threat-based prevention.

### 2.8 MAJOR — The scope of "Justice" is drawn differently in four entries
- Narrow (Justice, Self-Defense): Justice = disposal of moral debt only; restitution "independently"; self-defense "is not … Justice."
- Wide (Good, War): "good consists in Justice: stopping further violation, repairing material damage through restitution, and …" / "True defense against aggression is justice (stopping harm, restoring victims)."
- **Fix:** standardize on the narrow sense; reword Good and War to "stopping harm, Restitution, and Justice."

### 2.9 MAJOR — IOU classifies non-deceptive default as "fraud"
- IOU: "Breaking an IOU without cause is fraud." But Fraud requires **deception at acquisition**; a sincere promisor who later defaults performed no deceptive communication. The act exactly satisfies Contract Breach (restitution if damage), and the two classifications carry different consequences (Fraud invalidates consent, appears in Evil).
- **Fix:** "Breaking an IOU is Contract Breach owing restitution; it is Fraud only if the promise was insincere or deceptive when made."

### 2.10 MAJOR — Perimeter promises what Justice forbids, in Justice's own vocabulary
- Perimeter: "the certainty that **justice survives any atrocity**" and "no first strike can destroy the ability to **avenge**."
- Justice: for destroyed victims "no proxy is possible, no mandate can be granted" — Justice precisely does **not** survive a murder-atrocity. What survives is outlaw-ending, which is expressly not Justice (closes no debt).
- "Avenge" is defined nowhere; its nearest defined neighbor, Revenge, is condemned. Perimeter also uses "deterrence" for a mechanism its own Deterrence entry excludes (substituting the definiens yields "its attempt to prevent harm by threatening punishment is … not threat" — self-contradiction).
- **Fix:** reword Perimeter ("what survives any atrocity is the outlaw's total loss of protection"; "the ability to collect retribution"; "what deters is the certainty…"), and/or add the bridge entry for outlaw execution (see 3-G4).

### 2.11 MAJOR — Proportion's death ceiling cannot be legitimately collected
- Proportion: deep theft that costs lives → "death becomes the proportionate ceiling." But Punishment exists only "as directed by the victim or their proxy," and Justice says destroyed victims can mandate nothing. The victims whose deaths raise the ceiling are exactly the ones who cannot mandate it. Neither escape (aggregation of surviving victims' claims; outlaw route) is stated — and outlaw-ending is not punishment, while Proportion frames the death outcome as punishment.
- **Fix:** state the route in Proportion: lethally-consequential crimes make the offender an Outlaw (cross-reference), or victims'/heirs' mandates may aggregate to the act's full harm.

### 2.12 MAJOR — Wrongful punishment on later-disproven facts: guilt allocation missing
- Fallibility + revisable Judgments + irreversible sanctions, yet no entry addresses the executed-then-exonerated case. Derivable strict liability: a mandate to punish the wrong person is void ex ante → the good-faith punisher is "coercion with a new victim" (Mandate), guilt "objectively from causation," intention "does not erase harm" — the punisher of an innocent bears uncloseable guilt if the sanction was death. Whether guilt shifts to a lying accuser (as Duress shifts it for pressure) or an erring Judge (Error "is not evil" — if anything shielding the Judge while exposing the punisher) is nowhere stated. Hostile conclusion: no rational agent can ever safely act as punisher.
- **Fix:** a clause in Punisher or Judgment allocating responsibility for punishment executed on facts later disproven (deceiver who procured the mandate bears the guilt, parallel to Duress; state the due-care punisher's position; state what the wrongly punished are owed).

### 2.13 MAJOR — Outlaw status has no ceiling, procedure, or category
- Outlaw-ending closes no debt and needs no mandate → it is not Punishment → Proportion's ceiling does not govern it; total forfeiture silences the wrongness machinery for anything short of ending (torture, enslavement: neither licensed nor forbidden). Who declares the status, on what proof, whether it is contestable, and whether ending a person on a mistaken outlaw finding is murder — all absent. Largest exploitable surface in the file.
- **Fix:** outlaw status is a finding of Judgment on proven facts, contestable like any Judgment; forfeiture extends only to what the outlaw's own act rejected; mistaken ending allocates per the wrongful-punishment rule (2.12).

### 2.14 MAJOR — Subjective Value vs third-party sizing of restitution
- Value: "cannot be measured from outside" — yet a Judgment "states … what restitution and proportion follow," which is exactly an outside measurement. Damage's "material magnitude" is the intended reconciler but is never operationalized (replacement? market price? victim's declaration?), and Restitution's own genus re-imports "value" ("Returning stolen **value**").
- **Fix:** add "Exchange Value / Market Price" (intersubjective measure emerging from actual trades, distinct from subjective Value); reword Restitution to repair *damage*; state in Restitution/Judgment who fixes magnitude and by what standard.

### 2.15 MINOR — Vocabulary discipline in Perimeter (covered in 2.10) and the Proxy/Sovereignty/Aggressor gaps (Part 3).

## Part 3 — Missing entries (closure analysis, ranked)

**Critical (the machinery breaks without them):**
1. **Moral Debt** — used 12× across the justice cluster; no entry. Worse, the existing **Debt** entry ("What is owed when an IOU is given") actively conflicts with the moral sense.
2. **Forfeiture** — the mechanism that makes punishment non-criminal (Part 1).
3. **Sovereignty / Sovereign** — grounds Crime, Justice, Mandate, Murder, Self-Defense; Mandate's parenthetical is the only quasi-definition.
4. **Capacity / Capable (+ Child, + Guardianship)** — Kindness pivots on "capable agent" and licenses "guardianship," none defined; Consent has no capacity condition; the safeguard "do not treat capable adults as unable" is circular while "capable" is undefined; permanent incapacity falls outside Kindness's "temporary" license entirely; Agreement/Self-Ownership state no capacity carve-out.

**Major:**
5. **Proof / Proven (+ burden + standard)** — Judge and Judgment hinge on "proven facts"; Evidence is probabilistic while "proven" is a binary gate; nothing says how much probability constitutes proof, who bears the burden, or what standard precedes force (incl. the death ceiling).
6. **Heir / Estate / Inheritance** — no holder for a killed person's material restitution claim (the claim evaporates with the victim: "dependent" occurs only inside "independently"); also nothing on property at death or claims surviving death. Perimeter tacitly races the gap ("while their victims still live to mandate justice").
7. **Proxy** — carries the entire delegation structure; content scattered across Justice and Mandate.
8. **Aggressor** — used by Self-Defense and Proportion, undefined.
9. **Life / Death / Identity (continuity)** — Murder is "killing," Proportion's ceiling is "death," Good News promises "indefinite life," and the framework explicitly problematizes ordinary meanings (death "optional"); Pattern says agents are patterns and "none are permanent," yet Guilt must ride one persisting agent for decades — no entry says what makes a later pattern the same agent (copies, uploads, gradual repair: the exact question Good News turns on).
10. **First Acquisition / Homesteading (+ Abandonment)** — Property and Ownership are mutually circular for anything external to the body; creation and exchange presuppose owned inputs; no rule ever converts unowned land/resources into property. Theft over natural resources is undecidable; nothing distinguishes a state border from a vast private landholding's boundary (the Georgist attack and the "states homesteaded their territory" gambit both have no textual answer); rival first claimants cannot be adjudicated.
11. **Voluntary** (48 uses — the framework's most-used term), **Cooperation** (14 uses, carries Good News and Civilizational Velocity), **Manipulation** (a listed consent-invalidator in Consent, undefined), **Balance / "restore balance"** (Punishment's purpose-phrase, in tension with the debt model), **Ultimate Law** (named in the preamble as the alignment anchor, no entry), **Public space** (Privacy's carve-out rests on it; unresolved in a framework where all legitimate space is private), **Artificial scarcity** (condemnation criterion in four entries), **Expectation / Surprise / Problem / Probability** (Happiness is defined entirely through the first three; Evidence is defined purely through the fourth; the Happiness↔Curiosity reconciliation hangs on "unwanted" surprise).
12. **Agent-boundary criterion (Moral Agent / Moral Patient)** — Agent's definition plausibly admits dogs (making killing one Murder with uncloseable guilt) unless "agent" silently means Self-Awareness's "moral agent," which no entry says; Agency is thinner than Agent yet Rights derives everything from it; a conscious-but-not-self-aware being can be harmed but never consent, so every interaction with it "defaults to violation" (Agreement).

**Minor (short entries, mostly consolidation):** Offender; Vote/Voting ("vote cannot create consent" is a framework theorem); Tax/Taxation (condemned in four entries); Wealth; Body; Natural rights/natural law; Slavery; Flux; Labor; Inoculation Against Socialism; Externalizing costs (used in Evil); Collection & Release (used before/outside their inline gloss); Prosecution (used in Brand and Perimeter); economic primitives (Goods — note the equivocation hazard with the moral entry "Good" — Supply, Demand, Cost, Resource, Promise — the Money/IOU/Currency/Debt stack and Threat all rest on "promise," and nothing says whether a unilateral promise binds absent a Contract).

## Part 4 — Missing machinery (questions the system cannot currently answer)

- **G1. Third-party defense** of a victim under attack falls between Mandate's two branches (self-sourced = own boundary; proxy = post-harm mandate). A stranger's rescue-force has no stated basis.
- **G2. Conflicting Judgments under polycentric enforcement** — Perimeter mandates many competing enforcers with no monopoly; nothing resolves two contradictory Judgments over the same facts.
- **G3. Probabilistic / diffuse / not-yet-materialized harm** — pollution, drunk driving, risk imposition: no identifiable victim yet, or thousands of partial ones. "No victim no crime" as written cannot process them (Risk is defined but never connected to boundaries).
- **G4. Outlaw procedure** (= 2.13) and **execution bridge** (= 2.10).
- **G5. Animals / non-agent conscious beings** — either fork (full agenthood or no standing) is severe; neither is taken.
- **G6. Composite agents** — firms: on the current text (Collective: "no rights beyond the rights of its members") a company cannot own, trade, or bear liability; every entry speaks of individual agents.
- **G7. Good-faith purchaser of stolen goods** — title conflict machinery absent.
- **G8. Lesser Evil aftermath** — the entry names the category but not what follows: does the lesser-evil actor owe restitution to the victims they chose to create? (Consistency suggests yes; nothing says.)
- **G9. Wrongful punishment liability** (= 2.12).
- **G10. Time limits / expiry** — restitution claims and moral debts are apparently perpetual, clouding all title (compounds G7).
- **G11. Abandonment** — ownership is perpetual on the current text; no termination rule (compounds first-acquisition).
- **G12. Inheritance** (= Part 3 #6).
- **G13. Capacity determination** (= Part 3 #4): who decides, by what test, and how a guardianship claim is contested.

## Part 5 — Extended file (`coherent-dictionary-extended.txt`) is essentially unaligned

1. **Coverage: 12 of 190 terms (6.3%)** — the "aligned" block realigns only ~12 headwords; the remaining ~178 framework terms are absent from it.
2. **Stale definitions** — e.g. aligned "retribution": "Imposed suffering meant to balance harm already caused" — an older draft missing the victim/proxy/moral-debt model.
3. **Framework-critical headwords absent from the entire 52k-line file** (the newer coinages: Mandate, Outlaw, Proportion, Singleton, Perimeter, Way of Happiness, etc.).
4. **Encoding damage** — U+FFFD replacement characters in the aligned block, URL-encoded artifacts in the body.
5. **Unaligned WordNet leftovers** for framework-critical words (tax, government, justice, crime, punishment, authority…) still carry normative content contradicting no-victim-no-crime — the file is internally incoherent by the project's own standard.
6. **Inconsistent method** — some normative senses were silently deleted rather than realigned.
7. **Corrupted part-of-speech tags and duplicated definitions** (e.g. "knowledge [v. t.]", "war [a.]").

**Recommendation:** treat the extended file as a build artifact, not a source. Regenerate it from the simple dictionary + WordNet with a scripted pass once the simple dictionary is fixed — or drop it until then. Fixing it by hand before the simple dictionary stabilizes is wasted work.
